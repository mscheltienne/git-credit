An attribution is one author's share of one commit: name, email, credited additions and deletions, and `is_pr_author`, which is `true` only when the share comes from a [PR expansion](PR%20expansion.md). A regular commit has one attribution; an expanded squash merge has one per PR author.

Covered in: [Attribution fields](../Usage/02%20JSON%20Output.md#attribution-fields)
