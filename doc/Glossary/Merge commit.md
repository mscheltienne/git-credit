A merge commit is a commit with more than one parent. git-credit skips merge commits both in the walk and in PR commit lists, because the lines they bring in are already credited to the commits they merge.

Covered in: [Merge commits are skipped](../Attribution/01%20Commit%20Walk.md#merge-commits-are-skipped)
