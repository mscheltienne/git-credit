PR expansion is the replacement of a [squash-merge candidate](Squash-merge%20candidate.md)'s single author with the authors of the pull request's non-merge commits, fetched from the GitHub API, each credited a share of the squash commit's lines. An expanded commit is reported with `is_squash_pr: true`.

Covered in: [PR Expansion and Split](../Attribution/03%20PR%20Expansion%20and%20Split.md)
